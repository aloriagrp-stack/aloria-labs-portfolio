Architecture, Reliability & Data Security

How do you prevent hallucinations and ensure deterministic outputs in production?

We treat generative models as probabilistic reasoning components, not authoritative decision engines. To ensure deterministic reliability, we implement:

  • Strict JSON Schema Enforcement: Constrained decoding guarantees that model responses match strongly typed programmatic data contracts.
  • Deterministic Evaluation Harness: Systems are tested against 100+ automated edge cases before reaching staging or production.
  • Multi-Agent Verification Loops: Critical actions require an independent verification agent to inspect outputs against compliance policies.
  • Deterministic Fallback Handlers: If model confidence falls below parameterized thresholds, execution safely routes to deterministic fallback algorithms or human operators.
Where does our data live? Does Aloria Labs or foundation models train on our enterprise data?

Your data resides 100% inside your infrastructure. We engineer systems to deploy into your private AWS, GCP, Azure VPC, or on-premise hardware clusters.

We strictly enforce enterprise zero-data-retention agreements with foundation model providers and configure private open-weight models (such as Llama 3, Mistral, or Qwen) for sensitive workloads. Neither Aloria Labs nor external vendors ever use your internal business data or customer records to train public models.

How do you decide whether a problem requires AI or standard deterministic code?

We apply rigorous first-principles triage. If an operational bottleneck can be solved faster, cheaper, and with 100% certainty via standard relational databases, deterministic scripts, or rule-based cron workflows, we will explicitly advise against generative AI.

We only deploy autonomous AI agents when the task demands handling unstructured multimodal inputs (PDFs, voice, documents), dynamic multi-step cognitive reasoning, or ambiguous contextual decision-making.

IP Ownership, Legal & Engagement Terms

Who owns the intellectual property and code created during the engagement?

You own 100% of all intellectual property from day one. Every line of code, prompt hierarchy, system prompt, model adapter, database migration, and orchestration script is assigned directly to your entity.

We do not lock you into proprietary Aloria SaaS platforms or charge recurring runtime licensing fees. If you choose to bring maintenance in-house, your engineering team can run the entire codebase independently without any third-party friction.

Do you execute mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. Prior to reviewing your internal workflows, codebase, API documentation, or proprietary datasets, we execute a bilateral, enterprise-grade mutual NDA.

We treat your strategic initiatives, unit economics, and operational playbooks with top-tier confidentiality.

How long does an engagement take from kickoff to production deployment?

Our timelines are engineered for high-velocity execution:

  • Architecture Sprint: 10 to 14 business days from kickoff to validated working proof-of-concept and technical architecture blueprint.
  • Production System: 4 to 6 calendar weeks for full end-to-end builds, complete with internal API integrations, guardrails, and VPC deployment.
  • Systems Partner Retainer: Ongoing 3 to 12 month embedded engineering squad for continuous scaling and architectural evolution.
How does our internal team maintain the system after handover?

We engineer every system with self-documenting codebases, automated integration test suites, and strict typing.

At project delivery, we provide comprehensive architectural runbooks, telemetry dashboard configurations, and dedicated technical training workshops for your software engineers. Every production build also includes a 30-day post-launch warranty period for active bug fixes and performance optimization.
