HOW WE WORK

OUR PROCESS.

From initial audit to working prototype and production rollout in weeks. Clear milestones, no endless retainers, and 100% code ownership.

PHASE 01 WEEK 1

Discovery & Technical Blueprint

We review your current workflows, analyze where automation saves the most time and money, and design the exact technical blueprint before writing code.

PHASE DELIVERABLES
01

Workflow & Data Audit

We examine your tools, databases, and APIs to find high-ROI bottlenecks.

02

Cost & Time Projections

Clear estimates of hosting, API usage, and deployment timeline.

03

System Architecture Plan

Complete blueprint of the system and security safeguards.

PHASE 02 WEEK 2

Working Prototype & Testing

We build a working proof-of-concept using your real data so you can test it directly, give feedback, and verify results before full buildout.

PHASE DELIVERABLES
01

Working Demo Version

A live prototype you and your team can actually test and interact with.

02

Accuracy & Speed Benchmarks

Automated tests measuring accuracy, response speed, and edge cases.

03

Feedback & Review Call

Walkthrough to align on any tweaks before final production development.

PHASE 03 WEEKS 3–4

Production Build & Launch

We build out the complete system, connect your live tools, set up user permissions, and deploy it securely to your environment.

PHASE DELIVERABLES
01

Full Cloud Deployment

Deployed securely on your private cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure, or On-Prem).

02

Security & Access Control

Encrypted data, role-based logins, and automated error handling.

03

100% Code & IP Handover

You own all source code, model configurations, and documentation.

PHASE 04 POST-LAUNCH

Monitoring & Ongoing Support

We keep your system running smoothly with real-time monitoring, prompt updates, and direct engineering support whenever you need it.

PHASE DELIVERABLES
01

System Health Dashboard

Real-time stats tracking speed, usage, accuracy, and error alerts.

02

Model & Prompt Upgrades

Periodic tuning to keep outputs sharp as your business data evolves.

03

Direct Support Access

Direct communication channel with our engineers for fixes and updates.
