01 / PRIVACY POLICY

Information We Collect

Aloria Labs collects information you provide directly to us when you use our services, contact us, or submit a project inquiry.

04 / COOKIES

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies to ensure proper functionality and analyze website usage. We use:

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features but will not impact your ability to contact us.

05 / SECURITY

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

While we take every precaution, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use secure communication channels for sensitive information.
