05 / TECHNICAL INTELLIGENCE BRIEFING

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Essential clarity on architecture, security boundaries, hallucination prevention, and 100% client IP ownership.

How do you prevent hallucinations and ensure deterministic outputs in production?

We enforce strict JSON schemas, deterministic evaluation harnesses, tool execution sandboxes, and verification agent loops before systems commit high-stakes operations.

Where does our data live? Does Aloria Labs train on our proprietary data?

100% inside your perimeter. We deploy to your AWS, GCP, or Azure VPC. We enforce enterprise zero-retention API policies and private self-hosted open-weights models. Your data is never used for general model training.

Who owns the source code, prompts, and intellectual property?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, workflows, prompts, and model weights from day one. Zero vendor lock-in or recurring runtime licensing fees.
